Opal - The Expermental no code Tool

Google Opal is a visual, no-code prototyping tool that allows users to build "mini-apps" using natural language and drag-and-drop workflows. It democratizes AI development by letting non-engineers chain together prompts, models, and tools to create functional applications in minutes. Users can simply describe what they want the app to do, and Opal generates the necessary logic and interface. It serves as a rapid bridge between a raw idea and a shareable, working AI tool.Key FeaturesNatural Language Building: Describe an app in plain English to instantly generate its structure.Visual Flow Editor: Drag-and-drop interface to connect different AI models and logic steps.Instant Sharing: One-click deployment to share your mini-app with others via a link.Use CasesRapid Prototyping: Quickly testing a product idea before committing to full development.Internal Tools: Building simple workflow tools for HR or operations teams without engineering resources.Educational Aids: Teachers creating custom interactive tutors for specific lessons.
